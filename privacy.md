# Sort Privacy Policy

> **Draft under review.** This document is not yet in force. It is published here so it can be reviewed and referenced; the final version will replace it on this page.

**Version 3.0 · 14 October 2026**

## The short version (start here)

Sort helps you plan your time. To do that, we keep the things you put in: your tasks, when they are, and how you like to work.

- **We never sell your data. We never show you ads. We never build an advertising profile of you.** At any age.
- Your tasks are private. Nobody else on Sort can see them. Nothing is posted anywhere.
- Sort plans your day automatically. You can move anything, switch the working-style setting off, or ask a real person to look.
- You must be 14 or over. If you are 14 or 15 you get extra protection, and some features stay off.
- If you are 16 or 17, the law still counts you as a child, and the extra care still applies.
- The only phone permission Sort asks for today is notifications. Anything else is optional and off until you switch it on.
- You can delete everything from Settings. Your phone's copy goes at once. Our server copy is erased straight after, and we promise within 30 days.
- You can ask us what we hold, ask us to fix it, or complain to the ICO. Email **sort.app.v1@gmail.com**. You do not need to understand legal words to ask.

The rest of this policy is the detail behind those promises. It is one document on purpose, so there is one thing to keep true.

## 3. Your date of birth, in full

We ask once, at sign-up. It goes to our server, which works out your age band and discards the date. **There is no date-of-birth field in our database.**

**Your phone keeps one thing: the date you turn 16.**

That is how your account updates itself on your birthday, and how your Pro trial starts. We never hold your date of birth to do it.

On the day, your phone works the date out. It sends it once, so we can move you to the 16 and over band. Then it deletes it.

**The honest cost.** Until that day, the date sits in the app's ordinary storage, not an encrypted vault.

Anyone with your unlocked phone could work your date of birth back out. We think holding nothing on our servers is the better trade. You should know both halves.

## 5. Sort decides your schedule automatically

**Sort places your tasks automatically. No person reviews each placement.**

It uses fixed rules, plus a working style it works out from your setup answers. If you are 16 or over it also uses a motivation style.

**Working-style scheduling is on by default**. You can switch it off in Settings, and retake the setup quiz there too. With it off, Sort places tasks by the fixed rules alone.

Working out a style from your answers is a form of profiling, so we give you these rights:

- **Move any task yourself**, at any time. Nothing is locked.
- **Switch working-style scheduling off** in Settings.
- **Ask us to explain** why a task was placed where it was.
- **Ask a person at Sort to review it**, by emailing **sort.app.v1@gmail.com**. A human, not the engine, will look and change it if it is wrong.
- **Object** to being profiled this way, and we will explain what we can still offer if you do.

A placement in Sort has no legal effect on you. It is a suggestion you can change. We still treat it with the care above.

## 7. Where your data actually is

We will not claim Sort is UK-only, because it is not.

**Your tasks, habits, schedule, mood, focus and account records are at rest in the United Kingdom.**

Some Supabase access, support and backup activity may reach the United States.

**Your subscription record is different, and you should know it.** RevenueCat holds it in the **United States**. This affects **16 and over only**, because under-16s cannot subscribe.

Where data leaves the UK we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We also rely on the UK's adequacy findings for the EEA.

For RevenueCat we rely on those clauses alone. When we last checked, they were **not certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.**

The honest summary: **everything about how you plan your day is at rest in the UK. Your subscription status is in the US, under contractual safeguards only.**

## 8. How long we keep things

| Thing | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account and everything attached to it | Until you delete it |
| A task you delete | Gone from your schedule straight away |
| **The scheduling record of a task you deleted** | **Kept.** It contains what you typed, including the task title. **Deleting a task does not delete this record** |
| Mood entries and focus sessions | Kept for the life of the account. The app cannot yet delete them one by one |
| **Your student verification status** | Until it expires or you ask us to remove it |
| **Your subscription record at RevenueCat** | **Governed by their retention, not ours.** Deleting your Sort account does not automatically clear it. Ask us and we will make the request on your behalf |
| Diagnostics, if you switched them on | Each report is deleted automatically after a set period of no more than 30 days. Switching diagnostics off stops new reports at once |
| Your whole account, once you delete it | **Your phone's copy is wiped at once.** Our server erases your account record straight after, and everything attached to it goes with it. We promise this completes within 30 days, to leave room for a failed request to be retried |
| Backups of our database | **7 days** |

**On the scheduling record, plainly.** We kept those records to diagnose bad placements. We did not think through the consequence: the words of a deleted task outlive the task.

Our position, subject to our solicitor, is a retention limit of **90 days,** with an automatic purge.

Until that is built, email us to have every scheduling record erased. Or delete your account, and they go with it.

**On deletion, plainly.** A small marker stays on your phone after you delete your account. It stops old data creeping back into a new account. It holds an account code, not your tasks.

## 9. Optional features and phone permissions

Sort asks for **one phone permission today: notifications.** Everything below is off until you choose it, and we ask first.

| Feature | Status in this version | What it does when on |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** | On, if you allow it | Reminders scheduled on your phone. Change them in Settings or in your phone's settings |
| **Diagnostics** | Off by default. **16 and over only.** A switch in Settings | Sends the type of fault, your platform and the app build when the app crashes. Never your task words or error messages. Deleted automatically within 30 days. Switch off to stop |
| **Voice capture** | **Off for everyone** | Asks for the microphone. Each recording asks you to agree first. Sends the recording to OpenAI to turn into text, then the text to pick out the task. You check the result before anything is scheduled. **16 and over only** |
| **Calendar sync** | **Off for everyone** | Asks to read your phone calendar. Calendar entries can include other people's names and details, so you choose. **16 and over only** |

A permission listed in the app store is not the same as a feature being on. This table is what the app actually does.

## 10. If you are 14 or 15

We designed Sort for you as well, so this part is not an afterthought.

- **Your interests come first** when we make a design decision that affects you.
- **We check your age** at sign-up and hold you to your band. Under 14, no account.
- **We collect less from you.** No voice, no calendar, no motivation profiling, no diagnostics.
- **Your task words never go to an outside AI company.** That is true today, and we will tell you before it ever changes.
- **You cannot subscribe, and we never ask you to prove you are a student.** Nothing about you goes to RevenueCat or Student Beans.
- **Nothing about your data is used against you.** No advertising, no selling, no pressure to spend.
- **We do not nudge you.** No streak guilt, no "you will lose your progress", no dark patterns.
- **Sort works out a working style from your quiz answers**, and uses it to decide when things go in your day. You can switch that off in Settings. It is never used for adverts and never shared.
- **We write this in language you can actually read.** If any of it confuses you, email us and we will fix the wording.

**If you are 16 or 17**, UK privacy law still counts you as a child, and every point above still applies to you. The only difference is that Pro, student verification, voice and calendar become your choice, once they exist.

## 11. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- show you what we hold
- correct it
- delete it
- hand it over in a portable format
- stop or limit what we do with it
- reconsider an automated placement

Withdrawing consent does not undo what came before.

Email **sort.app.v1@gmail.com**. We will reply within one month. It is free.

If you are not happy, complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office**, at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first, but you do not have to.

## 12. What we do not do, and what is coming

**Today:** no advertising of any kind at any age. No advertising software, no analytics software. **No AI is used on anything you write.** The engine is fixed rules, the same way every time.

**Switched off:** voice capture and calendar sync are disabled in the app for everyone, at every age, right now.

**Planned, not built.** Voice capture would send your recording to OpenAI to turn into text, then the text to pick out the task details. For users 16 and over only.

**If you are 14 or 15, your content will not go to any third-party AI service.**

We will update this policy and tell you in the app first.

## 13. Changes

If we change this policy we will tell you in the app, and say what changed rather than just posting a new date.
