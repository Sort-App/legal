# Sort Cookie and Device Storage Policy

> **Draft under review.** This document is not yet in force. It is published here so it can be reviewed and referenced; the final version will replace it on this page.

**Version 1.0 · 14 October 2026**

## 1. What this is about

Cookies are small files a website puts on your device. Apps do something similar: they keep information on your phone so they work.

The same UK rules apply to both. We may keep what the service needs to work. For anything else, we ask first, and saying no must be as easy as saying yes.

This policy covers the Sort app and the website sortapp.co.uk. Our [Privacy Policy](privacy) explains what we do with your information more broadly.

This policy is issued by **SORT APP LTD**, company number 17375673. Questions to **sort.app.v1@gmail.com**.

## 2. The Sort app

**The app sets no cookies. It contains no advertising software and no analytics software.**

It keeps the following on your phone so it can work:

| What is kept on your phone | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your sign-in keys | To keep you signed in. Held in your phone's secure store | Until you sign out, or the session expires |
| Your tasks, habits, schedule and the queue of changes waiting to sync | So the app works offline and can recover if a sync fails | Until you delete them, or delete your account |
| Your settings: sleep window, categories, notification choices, working-style switch | To remember your choices | Until you change them |
| Your age band, and the date you turn 16 | To apply the age rules and update your account on your birthday. See the Privacy Policy, section 3 | Until your 16th birthday, or account deletion |
| Your diagnostics choice | To remember whether you switched crash reports on | Until you change it |
| Your trial, subscription and student verification status | To show the right features without asking the server every time | Until refreshed, or account deletion |
| A deletion marker | Kept after you delete your account, so old data cannot creep back into a new one. Holds an account code, not your tasks | Stays after deletion |

**Two things to know.** Your sign-in keys are in the secure store. Everything else is in the app's ordinary storage, which anyone with your unlocked phone could read.

**Optional features are off until you choose them.** Diagnostics, voice capture and calendar sync each ask you first. None of them is on in this version except diagnostics, which is a switch in Settings for 16 and over.

When the subscription screen opens, RevenueCat's software runs inside the app. **RevenueCat stores an anonymous App User ID and the cached status of your active subscriptions in the device's local secure storage. The purpose is to unlock Pro features even when the app is offline, and the retention period is until the app is uninstalled.**

The Privacy Policy explains what leaves your phone and who receives it.

## 4. Your choices

**In the app:** optional features are in Settings. Switching one off stops it at once. Deleting your account wipes the app's storage, except the deletion marker.

**On the website:** use the banner, or the Cookie settings link once it exists. You can also delete cookies in your browser, but that may sign you out of other sites and reset your choice here.

**Your phone's own settings** control notifications and, if ever switched on, the microphone and calendar. Those settings are separate from consent, and turning a permission on is not the same as agreeing to everything.

## 5. Changes

We will update this policy when the app or the website starts storing something new, and ask again where the law requires it. We will say what changed, not just post a new date.
