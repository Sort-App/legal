**Working-style scheduling is on by default. You can switch it off in Settings, and you can retake the setup quiz there too.**

If a placement is wrong, move it yourself in the app. Or email us.

**A real person will look at it, explain it, and change it if it is wrong.** You never have to accept an automatic placement.

Sort is a planning tool, not a guarantee. **You stay responsible for your own deadlines.**

## 12. Ending it

**The app wipes its copy from your phone straight away**. It then asks our server to erase your account, and everything attached to it goes with it. The app promises this completes within 30 days. It normally happens at once.

A small marker stays on your phone after deletion. It exists so old data cannot creep back into a new account. It does not contain your tasks.

Deleting your Sort account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. **Cancel that with Apple or Google separately**, or it keeps renewing.
